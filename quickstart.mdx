Bonfire Campus is a student social and schoolwork platform for students, teachers, and verified organizations. Use this page to learn how conversations, classroom tools, planning, and Focus fit together.

## Student conversations and planning

Use direct messages and group chats to connect with classmates. Bonfire Campus also brings classroom announcements, assignments, reminders, and calendar events into one place. Profiles let you add a photo and an About Me. From Messages, you can report a message or profile, block an account, or contact Bonfire Campus Support.

## Classes and school organizations

Teachers can use classrooms and group chats to share announcements and see when students have read messages. Verified organization admins can configure member settings and manage members within their own organization. Bonfire Campus staff verifies schools before organization access is enabled.

Google Classroom Sync is temporarily paused. Assignments already imported remain available in Assignments and Calendar.

## Bonfire Focus

Bonfire Focus is a Chrome extension for timed study sessions. After pairing it with your Bonfire Campus account, start a session from the extension popup for 5 to 120 minutes. Once the session is active, the timer cannot be ended early. A teacher can activate a session that a student has already armed. During a session, Focus filters ordinary website navigation to an education allowlist. An approved school website can request a temporary task window, and Focus returns to the saved tab when that window closes.

[Get Bonfire Focus from the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/oemmkpoejcekjpfefmgenpifdhcjakhj).

Focus is best-effort on unmanaged Chrome. A user can disable or remove the extension, quit Chrome, or use another browser or device. Focus does not collect general browsing history or page contents. Account eligibility is required to pair and use Focus.

For age-based messaging limits and reporting guidance, read the [Child Safety Policy](/child-safety-policy).
