## What do we do?

Bonfire Campus is a place where students in Middle School all the way to College can connect with their fellow students from the same school. Bonfire Campus also let's students create calendars and keep up with their school work. Schools or School District's can create an organization and connect Bonfire Campus to Google Workspace Directory or Microsoft 365 Directory to allow staff and student accounts to be automatically created.

**Bonfire Campus Activities**: Bonfire Campus Activities allows for students to join their club or activity groups to keep up to date and communicate with leaders.

**Bonfire Campus Alert**: Bonfire Campus Alert allows for teachers to communicate classroom announcements to students, while our system may also send assignment reminders and calendar updates.

## Bonfire Campus for Organizations/Schools

Bonfire Campus is compatible with school management, allowing for schools to enforce different settings on a user account. **Schools can verify their domain to force enlist accounts already created, the user's permission is not required.** Schools can also force log-in methods to Microsoft SSO or Google Workspace SSO, or both. **Custom client ids are not supported.**
